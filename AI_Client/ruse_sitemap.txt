The main course schedules are located under Academics > Student Resources > Timetables. It requires 4 clicks from the homepage.
The Erasmus+ mobility information and schedules are found under the International Relations tab on the top right navigation bar.
To change the website language to English, users must click the small flag icon in the absolute top-right corner of the header.
The Student Portal login for checking grades is a separate subdomain (portal.uni-ruse.bg) and cannot be accessed directly from the main homepage body, only the header menu.
If a user gets a 404 error on the homepage, it is usually because they clicked the legacy "News Archive" banner which has not been updated.
